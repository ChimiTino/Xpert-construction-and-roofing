Who We Are

Built on Experience & Trust

With over 15 years of experience, Xpert Construction & Roofing Services has grown from a trusted solar and electrical company into a leading construction and infrastructure partner — specialising in waterproofing, roof renovations, and repairs.

We deliver reliable solutions from minor fixes to complete overhauls. Our technical roots in solar and electrical installation give us a unique, practical understanding of how buildings work — allowing us to offer more integrated, lasting solutions than any ordinary roofing company.

Today we operate across six major regions in South Africa, and every project we take on is backed by the same commitment to quality, honesty, and efficiency that has defined us from the beginning.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

From solar panels to complete construction — our evolution has been driven by one thing: a commitment to solving our clients' real problems.

2008 – 2010

Founded as Solar & Electrical Specialists

Xpert was established as a solar and electrical services company, quickly building a reputation for technical excellence and reliable workmanship.

2011 – 2014

Expanding Into Roofing

Demand from clients led us to add roofing services. Our electrical background gave us an edge in solar-integrated roofing systems that few competitors could match.

2015 – 2018

Full Construction & Waterproofing Services

We expanded our service offering to include waterproofing, interiors, and general construction — becoming a one-stop construction partner for residential and commercial clients.

2019 – Present

Nationwide Footprint — 6 Regions

Today we operate across Cape Town, Johannesburg, Pretoria, Durban, Pietermaritzburg, Mpumalanga and Nelspruit — continuing to grow while keeping quality at the centre of everything we do.
